Enable an adaptive experience?

You have already consented to participate in this project.

An adaptive experience is different from a bounded experience. If you enable it, HumanOS may use your authorized interaction history within the governed experience to help determine which task you receive next.

This does not mean that HumanOS is making a diagnosis or assigning a fixed personal characteristic to you. The system remains subject to its governance rules and the limits of the experience.

You can decline and continue using a bounded experience instead.
