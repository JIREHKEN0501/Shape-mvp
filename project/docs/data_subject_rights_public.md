1. Introduction

HumanOS Tech is committed to protecting the privacy and rights of every participant — including adults, young people, and children using our tools under school or parental authorization.

This policy explains how any participant (or their parent/guardian or school administrator) may request:

Access to their data

Deletion of their data

Correction of inaccurate information

Withdrawal of consent

Export of their activity records

Our goal is to make these rights easy to exercise, simple to understand, and fast to fulfill.

2. Who Can Make a Request?
Adult Participants

You may request access, deletion, correction, or export of your personal data.

Parents or Guardians (Minors Only)

You may request access to or deletion of your child’s data at any time.

Schools or Organizations

If HumanOS Tech is deployed in a school, training program, or institution, the authorized school administrator may also request:

exports

deletions

confirmation of consent status

This ensures compliance with education and child-protection laws.

3. How to Submit a Request

You may request your data using one of the following methods:

1. Via API (for organizations)

/export/<participant_id>

/erase/<participant_id>

/withdraw/<participant_id> (coming soon)

2. Through Your School or Program

If HumanOS Tech is used in a school, contact the designated school administrator.
We will only process requests confirmed by the school to protect student privacy.

3. Via Parent/Guardian Email (for minors)

Parents may submit requests using the verified email address provided during onboarding.

4. Direct Request (adults only)

Adult participants may contact HumanOS Tech directly.

4. Types of Requests We Support
A. Access / Export

You may request a full copy of all data associated with a participant’s activity, including:

tasks completed

session data

performance metrics

consent record

Exports are delivered in a readable JSON format.

Fulfillment Time: Within 7 days

B. Deletion / Right to Be Forgotten

You may request the permanent removal of personal data such as:

participant ID

identifiers or mapping information

consent records

After deletion:

your personal identity is removed

your performance data becomes anonymous and cannot be linked back to you

schools/parents receive a deletion confirmation

Fulfillment Time:

72 hours for minors (COPPA requirement)

7 days for adults

C. Withdraw Consent

If you or your child no longer wishes to participate:

future data collection stops immediately

existing data can be deleted at your request

D. Correction Requests

If any information is incorrect, we will update or correct it promptly.

5. Verification Process

To protect participants — especially minors — we must verify every request.

Verification may include:

confirming participant ID

confirming parent/guardian email

school admin approval

confirming identity for adult users

This prevents unauthorized access or deletion of someone else’s data.

6. How We Protect Your Rights

HumanOS Tech follows major privacy laws, including:

NDPR (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation)

GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)

COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act)

FERPA (Student privacy laws for schools)

We only collect the minimum amount of data needed to provide the service, and we delete or anonymize personal information as soon as it is no longer required.
