## 1. Why We Keep Audit Logs

HumanOS Tech uses audit logs to:

- keep the system secure
- monitor for misuse or abuse
- verify that data is treated correctly
- support investigations if something goes wrong

Audit logs help ensure that the platform is used safely and responsibly, especially where children and students are involved.

---

## 2. What We Log

We log key actions such as:

- when a session starts
- when a task result is submitted
- when consent is given or withdrawn
- when an administrator views dashboards or exports data
- when there are unusual or suspicious activities (e.g., bots)

We **do not** log unnecessary personal details.  
We only log enough information to keep the system secure and accountable.

---

## 3. How Long We Keep Logs

Audit logs are kept for a limited time, as described in our Data Retention Policy.

In general:

- audit logs: kept up to 24 months  
- security-related logs: kept up to 90 days  

This helps us balance safety (for investigations and compliance) with privacy.

---

## 4. Who Can See Audit Logs?

Access to audit logs is strictly limited to:

- authorized technical staff
- security and compliance personnel

Schools **do not** receive raw audit logs by default, but they may receive summaries if needed (for example, to confirm that a deletion request was processed).

Audit logs are **never shared publicly**, and they are **never sold or used for advertising**.

---

## 5. How Audit Logs Protect Students and Participants

Audit logs help us:

- detect suspicious behavior
- verify that data exports and deletions were processed correctly
- ensure that only authorized admins access sensitive features
- maintain a provable record of how the system is used over time

For deployments in schools, this supports compliance with:

- NDPR and GDPR principles
- children’s online protection laws (e.g., COPPA)
- student records protection laws (e.g., FERPA)

---

## 6. Privacy Respect

Even in our logs, HumanOS Tech:

- avoids storing direct personal identifiers  
- uses pseudonymous IDs where possible  
- deletes or anonymizes data after our retention window expires  

Audit logs are used only to keep the system safe and fair — not to profile or punish individual students.
