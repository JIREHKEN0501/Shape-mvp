Purpose:
To explain how HumanOS Tech ensures reliable service availability and continuity during outages, maintenance, or unexpected events.

1. Overview

HumanOS Tech is designed to remain stable, secure, and accessible.
This plan outlines how:

we prevent downtime

we maintain availability

we recover from disruptions

we protect participant data

2. Key Principles

High Availability — services stay online with minimal interruption

Data Protection — participant data is protected during incidents

Safety for Minors — special integrity controls protect children’s participation

Transparent Communication — schools and parents are notified of any impact

3. What Happens if HumanOS Goes Down?

If a disruption occurs:

Step 1 — Detect Immediately
Our monitoring detects failures or errors in real-time.

Step 2 — Restore Service
Automated restart attempts run first.
If unsuccessful, our engineering team steps in.

Step 3 — Protect Your Data
Recent data is preserved using backups.
No participant data is lost or exposed.

Step 4 — Notify Institutions
Schools or partners receive an email status update
(e.g., service restored, under maintenance, data unaffected).

4. Backups & Recovery

HumanOS maintains:

Daily encrypted backups

Multiple stored copies (redundancy)

30-day rolling retention

Secure deletion of expired data

This ensures your participants’ progress is safe even during outages.

5. Planned Maintenance

For major improvements or updates:

maintenance windows are announced at least 24 hours in advance

maintenance is scheduled outside school hours when possible

dashboards display a temporary maintenance message

6. Unplanned Outages

If unexpected downtime occurs:

services auto-restart

engineering receives immediate alerts

no user action is required

institutions receive a brief summary after resolution

7. Commitment to You

HumanOS is committed to:

stable, safe, secure platform operations

protecting your participants' data

minimizing any learning disruptions

clear communication with schools and parents
