HumanOS Tech — Public Data Retention & Deletion Policy

Version: 1.0
Audience: Schools, Parents/Guardians, Participants

HumanOS Tech is designed with privacy, safety, and responsible data use at its core. This policy explains what information we collect, how long we keep it, and how you can request deletion or export.
We follow NDPR, GDPR, COPPA, and educational privacy best practices.

1. What Data Does HumanOS Tech Collect?
a. Participant Information

randomly generated participant ID

consent status (school or parental consent where required)

b. Task & Learning Data

answers to tasks

timestamps

difficulty level

performance progress patterns

c. System & Security Logs

session start

submit_result events

non-identifying error logs

security warnings (e.g., bot detection)

We do NOT collect names, emails, phone numbers, photos, or personal identifiers.

2. How Long Do We Keep Data?
a. Participant Identifiers

Retention: 30 days
Purpose: allow continuation of sessions
Afterward: permanently anonymized

b. Learning & Behavioral Data

Retention: 12 months
Purpose: progress tracking, insights, analytics
Afterward: anonymized (cannot be linked to individuals)

c. Aggregated Reports

Retention: up to 24 months
Note: never contains identifiable data.

d. Security & Audit Logs

Retention: 90 days
Automatically expires.

e. Backups

Retention: 30-day rolling window
Backups are encrypted and automatically deleted after aging out.

3. Your Rights (Parents, Schools & Participants)

You may request:

a. Data Export

A copy of all data linked to a participant ID.

b. Data Deletion

Full anonymization or removal of all records.

c. Withdrawal

We stop collecting any new data immediately.

Response time: within 72 hours.
