# HumanOS Tech — System Readiness Declaration

## Status
HumanOS MVP has completed Phase 7 (Validation & Interpretability).

The system is considered **safe for limited, supervised pilots**.

---

## Ready For:
- Educational pilots
- Research & exploratory analytics
- Human-in-the-loop evaluations
- Non-clinical, non-diagnostic contexts

---

## Not Approved For:
- Medical diagnosis
- Hiring/firing automation
- Psychological assessment
- Law enforcement or surveillance
- High-stakes autonomous decisions

---

## Preconditions for Use
- Explicit participant consent
- Clear communication of limitations
- Human review of outputs
- Compliance with applicable data laws

---

## Next Phase
Phase 8 focuses on:
- Pilot deployment
- Feedback loops
- UI/UX hardening
- Performance monitoring
