# Governance Contradiction Arbitration Matrix

## Purpose

This document defines constitutional precedence behavior
for conflicting governance conditions within HumanOS
runtime orchestration.

The goal is to prevent:
- governance contradiction ambiguity
- unstable authority restoration
- topology bypass escalation
- legitimacy corruption
- reevaluation deadlock
- replay inconsistency

Contradiction arbitration semantics define which
governance principles dominate when multiple
constitutional conditions conflict simultaneously.

## Constitutional Arbitration Principles

### Structural Legality Precedes Adaptive Recovery

Illegal governance topology transitions must never
be legitimized through confidence recovery,
temporal persistence, or reevaluation momentum.

### Restrictive Governance States Precede Restoration

Containment-oriented governance states dominate
authority restoration behavior until reevaluation
criteria stabilize sufficiently.

### Evidence Dominates Persistence

Temporal duration alone cannot establish legitimacy
without sufficient supporting evidence.

### Reevaluation Enables But Does Not Guarantee Recovery

Successful reevaluation may permit recovery
progression but does not override topology legality
or containment precedence semantics.

### Governance Explainability Must Survive Arbitration

Contradiction resolution behavior must remain
observable through governance traces and
constitutional reporting surfaces.

## Arbitration Matrix

| Conflict Condition | Dominant Principle | Arbitration Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| topology legality vs authority restoration | topology legality | illegal restoration blocked |
| containment vs unrestricted recovery | containment | restoration constrained |
| evidence insufficiency vs persistence | evidence validity | legitimacy denied |
| reevaluation success vs topology restriction | topology legality | restricted transition maintained |
| rehabilitation momentum vs low evidence | evidence validity | rehabilitation slowed |
| confidence escalation vs containment state | containment precedence | escalation constrained |
| reevaluation overdue vs governance persistence | reevaluation integrity | degradation triggered |
| explainability loss vs orchestration continuity | governance visibility | observability restoration required |

## Deferred Arbitration Areas

The following governance contradiction areas remain
intentionally deferred during Phase 3B.5 freeze
stabilization:

- multi-domain arbitration semantics
- replay-aware contradiction reconstruction
- adaptive legality inference
- autonomous topology mutation
- probabilistic governance negotiation
- historical causality arbitration
- distributed governance consensus

## Architectural Discipline

Contradiction arbitration semantics exist to preserve
constitutional coherence rather than maximize adaptive
behavioral complexity.

The governance runtime prioritizes:
- structural stability
- legality preservation
- auditability
- reversibility
- explainability

over adaptive optimization pressure.
