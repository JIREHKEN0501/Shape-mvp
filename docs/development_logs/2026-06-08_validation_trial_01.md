# Validation Trial 01

## Objective

Conduct the first evaluator-facing validation exercise using trajectory reconstruction artifacts generated from participant session data.

## Trial Design

Selected participant trajectories representing multiple outcome profiles:

* High performance trajectory
* Strong performance with measurable friction
* Low performance trajectory
* Recovery trajectory

Evaluators were provided observable evidence only.

No psychological, personality, diagnostic, or identity-based conclusions were permitted.

## Validation Principle

HumanOS evaluates observable behavioral patterns within task environments.

Patterns describe populations and sessions.

Patterns do not describe permanent characteristics of individuals.

## Trial Outputs

Trajectory artifacts included:

* Accuracy
* Hesitation
* Difficulty exposure
* Performance stability
* Challenge retention
* Observed friction
* Trajectory direction

All outputs remained grounded in observable session evidence.

## Key Findings

### Interpretability

Generated labels were understandable without requiring access to raw participant data.

### Consistency

Trajectory classifications aligned with human interpretation across tested examples.

### Evidence Traceability

All conclusions could be traced back to measurable session metrics.

### Limitation Discovery

The initial trajectory-state logic occasionally classified improvement patterns as volatility due to large accuracy ranges.

This finding directly motivated later trajectory dynamics calibration work.

## Outcome

Validation Trial 01 demonstrated that HumanOS can generate evaluator-facing trajectory summaries that remain:

* Interpretable
* Evidence-based
* Non-diagnostic
* Reproducible

The trial established the foundation for subsequent trajectory dynamics and recovery calibration work.
