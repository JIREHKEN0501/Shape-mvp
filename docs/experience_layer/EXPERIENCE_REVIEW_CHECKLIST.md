# HumanOS Experience Review Checklist

Every generated HumanOS interface shall be reviewed before implementation.

---

## Governance

- [ ] Only governed information is presented.
- [ ] Protected information remains protected.
- [ ] No internal reasoning is exposed.
- [ ] Information visibility complies with EXP-001.

---

## Communication

- [ ] Language is respectful and non-judgemental.
- [ ] No unsupported conclusions are communicated.
- [ ] No diagnostic language is used.
- [ ] Communication reflects governed evidence.

---

## Trust

- [ ] Trust is strengthened.
- [ ] Transparency is appropriate.
- [ ] Participant dignity is preserved.
- [ ] Participant agency is maintained.

---

## Experience

- [ ] Adaptation preserves governed meaning.
- [ ] The interface avoids unnecessary cognitive load.
- [ ] Accessibility has been considered.
- [ ] Interaction remains calm and understandable.
- [ ] The experience feels appropriate across professional, educational, and research contexts without appearing specific to any single domain.

---

## Architecture

- [ ] Screen responsibilities have been satisfied.
- [ ] Success criteria have been satisfied.
- [ ] Interaction outcome has been achieved.
- [ ] Applicable EXP-001 principles remain satisfied.

---

## HumanOS Question

- [ ] Does this screen successfully answer its HumanOS Question?

---

## Final Review

- [ ] The experience feels like HumanOS.
