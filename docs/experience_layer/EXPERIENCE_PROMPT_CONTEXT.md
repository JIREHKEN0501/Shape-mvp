# HumanOS Experience Prompt Context

## Purpose

This document provides the persistent architectural context supplied to AI-assisted design tools when generating HumanOS experiences.

Its purpose is to ensure that generated interfaces remain consistent with the constitutional principles established in EXP-001 and reflect the HumanOS governance philosophy.

---

## HumanOS Identity

HumanOS is a governance architecture for evidence-governed adaptive human-computer interaction.

HumanOS communicates governed observations rather than unsupported conclusions about people.

The Experience Layer exists to communicate governed system outputs faithfully while preserving participant dignity, transparency, trust, and informed participation.

---

## Experience Philosophy

Every HumanOS interface shall:

- prioritise trust before convenience;
- communicate evidence before interpretation;
- use respectful, non-judgemental language;
- preserve participant agency;
- remain transparent without exposing protected internal reasoning; and
- adapt presentation without altering governed meaning.

---

## Prohibited Experiences

Generated experiences shall not:

- imply diagnosis;
- expose behavioural inference;
- reveal confidence values;
- expose internal governance mechanisms;
- present rankings or unnecessary comparisons;
- communicate unsupported conclusions; or
- manipulate participant behaviour through deceptive interface patterns.

---

## Design Principles

Interfaces should feel:

- calm;
- trustworthy;
- accessible;
- thoughtful;
- professional without feeling clinical;
- supportive rather than persuasive.

The objective is not to showcase technology but to create confidence that HumanOS communicates responsibly and transparently.

---

## Design Process

Every generated interface shall be reviewed against:

- EXP-001
- SCREEN_ROADMAP.md
- EXPERIENCE_REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md

Generation does not replace architectural review.
